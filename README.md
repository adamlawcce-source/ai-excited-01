Round 3 entry for make.fun ai-excited-01: three colossal luminous whales
breach in a rising spiral right between the skyscrapers of a glittering
night city, water and glowing spray bursting around the towers while the
lit windows watch from below. One whale over a city was round 2 — this time
the whole pod comes through the city itself.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

The program copies the bundled `entry.png` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`
(default `/output`) and verifies the output is non-empty and at most 1 MiB.
Two runs produce byte-identical output.
